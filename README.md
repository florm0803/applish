# applish ✦

A personal space to explore, practice, and improve my English, one word at a time.

Applish is a growing vocabulary library designed to make learning English more practical, intuitive, and enjoyable.

It brings together everyday vocabulary, phrasal verbs, idioms, and natural expressions used by native speakers.

## What you'll find

- **Vocabulary:** Verbs, nouns, adjectives, adverbs, and more.
- **Phrasal verbs:** Common expressions and their meanings.
- **Real-life examples:** Learn how words are used in context.
- **Categories:** Explore vocabulary by topic and difficulty level.
- **Favorites:** Save words and expressions for later.
- **Practice mode:** Review and reinforce what you've learned.

## The idea behind applish

Learning a language is more than memorizing words. It's about understanding how people actually communicate.

Applish is my little corner of the internet to keep learning, stay curious, and build my English vocabulary along the way.

**Learn it. Save it. Use it. ✦**
